## Welcome to Slack

This is a general document about how to use Slack. But Slack has *really good documentation* online, so rather that reproduce all that, we'll point to the documentation there since it's likely to stay up to date.

### Slack at NSPS

Slack is a platform to chat to other people at NSPS. It's a private place just for members. 

* Email addresses are not shared.
* Non-members cannot access it.
* Photographs that are shared are not visible by the public.
* Photographs that are shared are stripped of [EXIF information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exif).

### Joining Slack

Contact anyone on the board, or [Andy McKay](andy@mckay.pub) or Ronia Nash and we can get you access. You'll get an email. Click on the links in the email and follow the setup process to get an account.

Slack is completely free and can be used on the web or with apps on iPhone, Android, and Desktops. Any of those work and data is automatically synced across them all.

* [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/slack/id618783545)
* [Android App Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Slack&listing&hl=en-US)
* [Desktop for Windows](https://slack.com/downloads/windows)
* [Desktop for Mac](https://slack.com/downloads/mac)
* [Desktop for Linux](https://slack.com/downloads/linux)
* [Web Version](https://slack.com/)

### Getting help

Slack is divided into channels (denoted with a leading `#`) and people can chat in those channels, or just to each other.

Once you are in, you will be automatically added to the `#slack-help` channel. This channel is the perfect place to ask any questions about Slack and how to use it. We'll try our best to help.
